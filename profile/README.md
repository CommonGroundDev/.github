# CommonGround

### Building the Trust Infrastructure for AI

CommonGround is developing enterprise software that turns AI evaluation science into dependable tools for real-world use.

Our goal is to help enterprises and independent testing labs put credible measurement to work: defining what to test, running repeatable evaluations, understanding results, and producing evidence that supports decisions about AI systems.

## Why CommonGround?

AI evaluation is a growing field, with valuable work spread across research groups, standards organizations, testing labs, and software companies. Yet teams often have to assemble their own tools, repeat integration work, and reconcile results produced using different methods.

More testing alone does not solve that problem. Teams need reliable evaluation methods, usable software, and a clear connection between requirements, tests, and evidence.

CommonGround brings together experience in AI benchmark development and enterprise software delivery to help close that gap.

## What we are building

Our product direction connects four parts of AI trust infrastructure:

- **Benchmarks and datasets** grounded in credible evaluation science.
- **Evaluation frameworks** for repeatable testing, scoring, and analysis.
- **Enterprise software** that integrates evaluations into customer workflows.
- **Continuous AI assurance** through recurring testing, results tracking, and reporting.

Our initial focus is safety and security evaluation for AI systems, including jailbreak resistance and agent behavior. We aim to translate customer and standards requirements into practical tests and evidence that enterprises and independent labs can use.

## How we work

We intend to build on established research, benchmarks, standards, and tools, while developing the software and capabilities needed to make them useful in enterprise settings.

That means focusing on:

- **Reproducibility:** documented methods, versions, configurations, and scoring.
- **Useful evidence:** results tied to defined requirements and evaluation scope.
- **Validated measurement:** checking evaluation quality against ground truth and understanding its limitations.
- **Enterprise delivery:** integration, deployment, maintenance, and support.
- **Collaboration:** working with researchers, tool builders, standards organizations, and testing labs.

## Creating common ground

Our long-term ambition is to make the AI trust ecosystem’s collective work more effective.

Through shared interfaces, reusable evaluation components, and clear evidence structures, CommonGround aims to connect complementary efforts and reduce redundant work—while preserving specialist expertise and independent judgment.

**We are creating common ground for AI trust infrastructure.**
